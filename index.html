Privacy Policies

Eleven published apps · one policy page each · last reviewed 25 November 2026

None of the apps listed here has an account, a developer-run server, analytics, crash reporting or third-party tracking of any kind. In no case does any information about you reach the developer. That is not a promise about intent — it is a property of the builds themselves, and the sections below say how to check it yourself.

Seven of the eleven carry no advertising either. জমি হিসাব, ঠাকুরঘর, Onkoo and প্রস্তুতি are the exceptions: they show Google AdMob ads, which send your advertising ID and similar device data to Google. Rather than bury that, their pages state it in the opening paragraph and devote a full section to what is sent and how to switch personalisation off.

ঠাকুরঘর also offers membership and one-time purchases through Google Play, and can use your approximate location — only if you switch it on, once, on the phone, to pick the nearest city. The coordinates are never stored or sent.

Inked is the one with online features, and each is optional: Google Play Games leaderboards (your daily times and streaks, posted to Google if you sign in to Play Games), an Editions subscription bought through Google Play, and cloud save to your own Google Drive. None of it reaches the developer.

Onkoo, the number-puzzle game for grown-ups, shows rewarded ads only (a video you choose to watch, never a banner or pop-up), and none at all to Onkoo Plus owners. Its optional Google Play Games sign-in posts your XP to a weekly league and can keep a cloud save in your own Google account.

Summa, the calculator, works entirely on the phone. Its only online features are Google Play, for the optional one-time Summa Plus purchase, and an optional backup of your history and settings to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. Neither reaches the developer.

প্রস্তুতি (Prostuti), the exam study planner, keeps your syllabus, routine and scores on the phone. Its free version shows AdMob banners on four screens; Prostuti Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play; and an optional backup goes to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. It also downloads the latest exam dates from a public Google Sheet, a request that carries nothing about you. None of it reaches the developer.

Onkoo Kids is the one made for children (ages 4–8). It collects nothing, works offline, and keeps its optional tip jar, its rating button and the developer's links behind a parental gate that a young child can't pass.

Each policy describes exactly what its app does with data: which Android permissions the released build actually declares, what is written to your device and where, which servers (if any) it contacts, and what happens to anything you choose to export or share.

There is no deletion request to file with the developer, because the developer holds no copy of anything to delete — uninstalling an app removes everything it ever saved. The things outside that guarantee are held by Google, not the developer: the ad data it receives from জমি হিসাব, ঠাকুরঘর, Onkoo and প্রস্তুতি, which Android's own advertising-ID controls let you reset or delete, and the Play Games scores and cloud saves of Inked and Onkoo, which you can delete from your Play Games profile, and the Drive backups of Summa and প্রস্তুতি, which you can delete from Google Drive.

Checking any of this yourself

A privacy policy is a claim. These particular claims are testable, and you do not have to take the developer's word for any of them:

  • Permissions. On your phone, open Settings › Apps › [the app] › Permissions. The Play listing also shows the full declared list under About this app › App permissions › See more.
  • Airplane mode. GPA Calculator BD and CNG দরদাম have no internet permission at all in their released builds. শব্দ খেলা declares one, but uses it for nothing except the cloud save you switch on yourself — leave that off, turn on airplane mode, and every feature still works, because none of it was ever coming from a server. জমি হিসাব declares one solely for its ad banner: in airplane mode every conversion, plot area and হিস্যা split still works, and only the banner goes missing.
  • The APK itself. If you have the Android SDK, aapt2 dump permissions app-release.apk prints the complete permission list of a build. জমি হিসাব's page goes further and names the library that contributes each of its eight entries, so the list can be checked line by line.
