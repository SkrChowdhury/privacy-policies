Checking any of this yourself

A privacy policy is a claim. These particular claims are testable, and you do not have to take the developer's word for any of them:

  • Permissions. On your phone, open Settings › Apps › [the app] › Permissions. The Play listing also shows the full declared list under About this app › App permissions › See more.
  • Airplane mode. GPA Calculator BD and CNG দরদাম have no internet permission at all in their released builds. শব্দ খেলা declares one, but uses it for nothing except the cloud save you switch on yourself — leave that off, turn on airplane mode, and every feature still works, because none of it was ever coming from a server.
  • The APK itself. If you have the Android SDK, aapt2 dump permissions app-release.apk prints the complete permission list of a build.
