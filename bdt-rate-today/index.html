Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 August 2026

The short version: no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads. The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal information about you. There is nothing to delete, because nothing is kept.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no login, no profile, no contact form and no advertising. The app requests only one Android permission — internet access — and uses it solely to fetch exchange rates.

Your choices inside the app (which currency corridor you are viewing, the amount you typed, light or dark theme) live in memory for as long as the app is open. They are not written to disk, not sent anywhere, and not tied to any identifier. Closing the app discards them.

Rate data and third-party services

To show live comparisons, the app requests publicly available exchange-rate data from the sources below. These are ordinary HTTPS requests for public rate information — the app sends no personal data, no device identifier and no account with them.

ServiceUsed for
Wise Comparisons API
api.wise.com		Provider-by-provider transfer quotes
bdt-rates.com

Open Exchange Rates

As with any request your device makes to any website, the operators of these services can see the network-level information every HTTPS request carries — your IP address and the general shape of the request. That is handled under their own privacy policies, not this one, and we neither receive nor store it.

Backup

The app disables Android Auto Backup, so none of its data is copied to your Google Drive. Since it stores nothing in the first place, there would be nothing to copy.

Links out to providers

Tapping “Send with…” or a provider row opens that provider's own website in your browser. Once you leave the app you are on their site, under their terms and their privacy policy. No tracking or referral identifier is appended to those links.

Rates are indicative

Quotes come from public sources and include each provider's transfer fee, so they reflect what actually arrives rather than a headline exchange rate. They can still go stale or differ from what a provider finally offers. Always confirm on the provider's own site before sending money. The app displays information; it is not a financial service and does not process payments.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.
