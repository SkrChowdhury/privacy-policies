1. What the app collects

Nothing. There is no login, no profile, no contact form, no newsletter and no advertising. The app has no server of its own, so there is nowhere for information about you to be sent even in principle.

Specifically, the app never collects, requests or receives:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail
  • the amounts you type, the corridors you look at, or any record of your searches
  • your location, at any precision
  • your device identifiers, Android ID or advertising ID
  • analytics, usage statistics, session recordings or crash reports
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone
  • anything at all about a transfer you go on to make — the app is never told about it

2. What is stored on your device

Nothing is written to storage. This app is unusual among the three published here: it has no on-device database and no persisted settings file. It bundles no key-value store, no SQLite database and no preferences library.

Your choices inside the app — the currency corridor you are viewing, the amount you typed, whether you are in light or dark mode — exist only in the app's memory while it is running. Fetched rates are held in the same way, in a short-lived in-memory cache, so that switching between corridors you have already opened is instant instead of re-fetching. That cache is cleared when you pull to refresh, and it disappears entirely when Android closes the app.

Close the app and none of it survives. Reopening starts from a blank slate and fetches fresh rates.

3. Permissions

The released build declares exactly one Android permission:

PermissionWhy it is needed
android.permission.INTERNET To fetch public exchange-rate data. This is the app's entire purpose; without it there are no rates to show.

INTERNET is an install-time permission that grants network access and nothing else. It does not give an app access to your files, your location, your accounts or any other information about you, and Android does not prompt for it because there is nothing sensitive to grant.

The app requests no storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, notification or background-activity permission.

4. Network requests

The app makes requests to four public rate sources, and to nothing else. Every request is a plain, unauthenticated HTTPS lookup for public pricing information. There is no API key tied to you, no session, no cookie and no device identifier.

HostUsed forWhat the request contains
Wise Comparisons API
api.wise.com		 Provider-by-provider transfer quotes for a corridor Source currency, target currency (BDT) and the send amount
bdt-rates.com
dynamic-whale-748.convex.cloud		 Fee-inclusive rates for the corridors it covers The corridor being viewed
Remitly
api.remitly.io		 Quotes for the AED corridor The corridor and the send amount
Open Exchange Rates
open.er-api.com		 Mid-market reference rate Nothing beyond the request for the public USD rate table

The amount sent is a plain number used to price a quote — the same number anyone would type into a public rate calculator on the web. It is not attached to your name, your device or any identifier, and the app keeps no record of it.

As with any request your device makes to any website, the operators of these services can see the network-level information that every HTTPS request inevitably carries: your IP address and the general shape of the request. That is handled under their own privacy policies, not this one. The developer neither receives nor stores it, and has no account or dashboard with any of them.

There is no advertising network, analytics SDK, attribution SDK, crash reporter, A/B testing service, push-notification service or social-media SDK in this app.

6. Libraries in the app

The app is built with React Native and bundles a deliberately short list of open-source libraries: a gradient renderer, an SVG renderer and a safe-area helper for handling notches and gesture bars. None of them contacts a server, and none collects data. There is no dependency in the build whose purpose is to observe the user.

7. Backup

The app sets android:allowBackup="false", so Android's Auto Backup does not copy any of the app's data to your Google Drive. Since the app stores nothing on the device in the first place, there would be nothing to copy — the flag simply makes that explicit to the operating system.

8. Links out to providers

Tapping “Send with…” or a provider row opens that provider's own website in your browser. Once you leave the app you are on their site, under their terms and their privacy policy.

No tracking parameter, affiliate tag or referral identifier is appended to those links. The app is not an affiliate of any provider it lists, earns nothing from a tap, and is not told whether you went on to send money.

9. Retention and deletion

Nothing is retained, so there is nothing to request the deletion of. No personal data exists in any system controlled by the developer. Uninstalling the app removes the app; there is no residue anywhere else and no account left open.

10. Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR give you rights of access, correction, export and erasure over personal data a service holds about you. Those rights are fully satisfied here in the simplest possible way: no personal data is collected, so there is no profile to access, nothing to correct, nothing to export and nothing to erase. The app has no legal basis to establish because it performs no processing of personal data.

If you believe any of that is untrue of a build you are running, please write to the address below — that would be a defect, and it would be fixed.

11. Verifying these claims

You do not have to take this page's word for it:

  • Open Settings › Apps › BDT Rate Today › Permissions, or the Play listing's About this app › App permissions › See more. Internet access is the only entry.
  • Watch the app's traffic with any network inspection tool. Every request goes to one of the four hosts listed above, and none carries an identifier.
  • With the Android SDK installed, aapt2 dump permissions app-release.apk prints the build's complete permission list.

12. Rates are indicative

Quotes come from public sources and include each provider's transfer fee, so they reflect what actually arrives rather than a headline exchange rate. They can still go stale, or differ from what a provider finally offers you once its own checks are done. Always confirm on the provider's own site before sending money.

The app displays information. It is not a bank, a money-services business or a financial adviser, it does not process payments, and it holds no funds.

13. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included. It shows no ads, contains no in-app purchases, has no chat and hosts no user-generated content.
