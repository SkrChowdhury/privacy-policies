Jomi Hishab terms

The short version: জমি হিসাব converts land units, works out plot areas, prices, lengths, sums and shares, and keeps a ledger of the results you save. Every one of those calculations runs on your phone, offline, and none of your land data ever leaves it — no account, no server, and the numbers you type are never sent anywhere.

Three things do use the network, and this page describes each exactly: ads from Google AdMob (which may fill them through Meta Audience Network or AppLovin), usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase, which receive a few named events and never your numbers, and Google Play Billing, if you buy the one-time "remove ads" purchase.

1. What the app collects

Nothing you enter into the app ever leaves your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no server behind this app.

In particular, the app never sends anywhere:

  • your land measurements, plot dimensions, prices, হিস্যা shares or any result the app calculates for you
  • anything in your ledger (জমির খাতা) — saved results, their names and notes
  • your own custom units, the values you adjust, or the region you pick
  • your name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail
  • your location, at any precision
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone

What does leave the phone is described in the next three sections: ad requests made by the ad networks' SDKs, a handful of usage events sent to Google Analytics for Firebase, and, only if you buy it, a purchase through Google Play. None of them carries any of the above.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: native ad cards on a few screens, a banner on three screens, and a rewarded video that plays only when you choose to watch one (for 24 hours without ads, or for an HD result image). New users see no ads at all for their first sessions.

AdMob may fill an ad through a mediation partner: Meta Audience Network or AppLovin. When it does, that network's SDK in the app requests and shows the ad.

What the ad networks receive

To select, serve and measure ads, Google and, when they serve an ad, Meta or AppLovin may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID — a resettable identifier Android provides specifically for advertising
  • device information such as model, operating-system version, language, screen size and network type
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address — typically city-level, and not the device location permission, which this app does not have and never requests
  • ad-delivery events such as whether an ad was shown, tapped or a video watched to the end
  • diagnostic information about the app and the ads SDK, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use

This goes to the ad networks, not to the developer. The developer sees only aggregate earnings in the AdMob dashboard — never anything about an individual person or device.

How each handles this data: Google, Meta, AppLovin.

What the ad networks do not receive

Ad requests carry no data from inside the app. Your measurements, results, ledger, saved units, region, language and theme are never attached to them.

Turning ad personalisation off

  • Settings › Google › All services › Ads › Delete advertising ID — after this the app still shows ads, but no network has a stable identifier with which to personalise them
  • on older Android versions the same screen offers Opt out of Ads Personalisation instead
  • inside the app, Settings › বিজ্ঞাপন সরান removes ads for 24 hours (after a video) or for good (with the purchase in section 4)

The app keeps working exactly as before either way. Every calculation is offline, so nothing depends on an ad loading.

Android's Privacy Sandbox

The Google Mobile Ads SDK declares the Android Privacy Sandbox permissions — ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS — on every app that includes it. They are listed in section 7 with everything else.

Along with these, Firebase records its own standard information: an app-instance ID generated on install, the advertising ID, device model, operating-system version, app version, a coarse location derived from a masked IP address, and its automatic events — first open, sessions, app updates and, if you buy "remove ads", the purchase with its product ID and price. 4. Purchases

"চিরতরে বিজ্ঞাপন সরান" (remove ads forever) is an optional, one-time purchase made through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment and holds your payment details; the developer never sees them.

The app learns only whether the purchase exists, so it can keep ads off — including after a reinstall, when it asks Google Play for your purchase history. Play's own records of the purchase are governed by Google's privacy policy.

6. Files you save or open

  • Backup. খাতা › ব্যাকআপ writes one JSON file — your ledger, custom units, adjusted values, pins and region — to a place you pick in Android's own save dialog. Restoring reads a file you pick in Android's file picker. The app sees only the file you choose.
  • Result images. ডাউনলোড saves a picture of a result to Pictures/JomiHishab on your phone. On Android 9 and older this needs the storage permission, which the app asks for at that moment; newer Android versions need no permission for it.

Nothing here is uploaded. A backup file goes wherever you put it — if you save it to a cloud folder, that service's policy applies to the copy.

8. Network access

The app's own code makes no network requests: searching its source for fetch, XMLHttpRequest or WebSocket returns nothing. All traffic comes from the SDKs in sections 2–4: ad requests (Google, and Meta or AppLovin when they fill an ad), Firebase Analytics and Remote Config (Google), and Play Billing (Google).

Put the phone in airplane mode and everything still works. Every unit, regional কানি, formula, calculator, the ledger, backup and the result images are on the device. Offline, you lose only the ads, the statistics and the ability to buy "remove ads".

9. Libraries in the app

The app is built with React Native and bundles open-source libraries for animation, vector drawing (SVG and Skia, for the result image), safe-area layout, local storage, clipboard and sharing, along with the Bengali and Latin typefaces it renders text with. None of them contacts a server.

The libraries that use the network are:

  • Google Mobile Ads (react-native-google-mobile-ads, wrapping play-services-ads) — section 2
  • Meta Audience Network and AppLovin mediation adapters for AdMob — section 2
  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Remote Config (@react-native-firebase) — section 3
  • Google Play Billing (react-native-iap) — section 4

There is no crash reporter, push-notification service, social-media login or third-party attribution SDK.

10. Clipboard and sharing

Tapping a result copies that number to your clipboard, and "শুধু লেখা কপি করুন" copies a result's text. The app never reads your clipboard.

Sharing a result hands an image and its text to Android's share sheet; it goes only to the app you pick there. The developer never learns what you shared, or with whom.

11. Android Auto Backup

Android's Auto Backup is switched off for this app (android:allowBackup="false"). Your ledger, units and preferences are never copied to your Google account or a phone-to-phone transfer. To move them to a new phone, use the app's own backup file (section 6), which you control.

12. Links that leave the app

The developer screen has two links to public profiles — LinkedIn and skrchowdhury.online. Tapping one opens it in your browser, with no app data attached.

13. Retention and deletion

  • deleting a ledger entry, a custom unit or an adjusted value inside the app removes it immediately
  • Settings › Apps › জমি হিসাব › Storage › Clear data, or uninstalling, removes everything the app stored
  • backup files and saved images are ordinary files: delete them like any other

The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request. Data the ad networks and Firebase hold is governed by their own retention and controls. Firebase Analytics data is kept for Google Analytics' standard retention period, is tied to the app-instance ID rather than to you, and is reset when you clear the app's data or reinstall.

14. Security

Your settings and ledger are protected by the Android application sandbox: readable only by this app on an unmodified device. There is no additional encryption layer, and none is claimed. The SDKs that use the network connect over their own encrypted connections.

15. Your rights

Laws such as Bangladesh's and India's data-protection rules and the GDPR give you rights over personal data a service holds about you. The developer holds none. For advertising and analytics data, the controls are Android's and Google's: delete your advertising ID (section 2), clear the app's data to reset the Firebase app-instance ID, and use your Google account's own controls.
