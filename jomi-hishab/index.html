The short version: জমি হিসাব converts land units, works out plot areas and splits হিস্যা shares. Every one of those calculations runs on your phone, offline, and the developer receives nothing — there is no account, no server and no analytics.

1. What the app collects

The app itself collects nothing, and the developer receives nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no server behind this app and no code in it that sends your data anywhere.

In particular, the app never collects, requests or receives:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail
  • your land measurements, plot dimensions, হিস্যা shares or any result the app calculates for you
  • your own custom units, the values you adjust, or the region you pick
  • your location, at any precision
  • analytics, usage statistics, diagnostics or crash reports
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone

Every unit factor, every regional কানি definition and every formula lives inside the app package on your phone. When you type a number, it is converted on your device and displayed on your device. Nothing about the land you are measuring is reported to anyone.

The one thing that does leave your phone is the ad request, and it carries none of the above. It is described next.

2. Advertising

The app shows a single banner from Google AdMob. This is the only part of জমি হিসাব that uses the network, and the only reason the app declares an internet permission at all.

What Google receives

To select, serve and measure that banner, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID — a resettable identifier Android provides specifically for advertising
  • device information such as model, operating-system version, language and screen size
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address — typically city-level, and not the device location permission, which this app does not have and never requests
  • standard ad-delivery events such as whether a banner was shown or tapped

This goes to Google, not to the developer. The developer has no server, receives no copy of it, and sees only aggregate earnings figures in the AdMob dashboard — never anything about an individual person or device.

Google's own description of how it handles data from apps using its ad services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and its advertising policies at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

What Google does not receive

The ad request carries no data from inside the app. Your measurements, your results, your saved units, your region, your language and your theme are never attached to it — they are not sent to Google, they are not sent anywhere, and the app contains no code that could attach them.

Turning ad personalisation off

You can stop personalised advertising at the Android level, for this app and every other one on the phone:

  • Settings › Google › All services › Ads › Delete advertising ID — after this the app still shows ads, but Google no longer has a stable identifier with which to personalise them
  • on older Android versions the same screen offers Opt out of Ads Personalisation instead

The app keeps working exactly as before either way. Every calculation is offline, so nothing you use জমি হিসাব for depends on the banner loading.

Android's Privacy Sandbox

Recent versions of the Google Mobile Ads SDK declare the Android Privacy Sandbox permissions — ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS. These are part of Google's replacement for cross-app identifiers and are declared by the SDK on every app that includes it, this one included. They are listed in section 4 with everything else, so that the permission list on this page matches the one your phone shows.

3. What is stored on your device

So that the app remembers how you like it, the following is saved in the app's own private storage — a single AsyncStorage entry under the key jomihishab.settings.v1, in the app's private data directory, which the Android sandbox makes unreadable to every other app on the phone:

What is savedDetail
Region Which regional definition of কানি and গন্ডা to use, so the arithmetic matches your area
Language and theme Bengali or English, light or dark
Pinned units Up to three units you want shown first
Your own units Any custom unit you create, with the name and square-foot value you gave it

That is the complete list. The numbers you type and the results you get are not saved at all — there is no history, no recent-calculations list and no log. Close the app and they are gone.

This data belongs to you. It is readable only by this app, is never transmitted, and is removed when you clear the app's data from Android Settings or uninstall the app.

4. Permissions

The released build declares nine permissions. Exactly one is written by the app; the rest are merged in by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the libraries it depends on, and AndroidX. The table names the origin of each, because a permission list is only meaningful if you can tell who asked for it and why.

If you inspect the manifest of the released build you will also see a ninth entry, com.skrchowdhury.jomihishab.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. That one is generated automatically by an Android system library. It is signature-level — only a build signed with this app's own key could ever hold it — it is scoped to this app alone, and it grants access to nothing. It exists so the app's internal message receivers are not exposed to other apps, which is a privacy protection rather than a privacy cost. There is no location, storage, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, notification or vibration permission. Nothing in that list can read a file you own, see where you are, or identify you personally.

A copy of the app's manifest also carries a com.google.firebase.messaging.default_notification_icon entry, which points at a drawable. It is a leftover reference to an icon resource, not a permission and not a messaging service: the app has no Firebase or push dependency and cannot send you a notification. It is noted here only so the manifest holds no surprises.

5. Network access

The app's own code makes no network requests of any kind. There is no HTTP client, no socket and no analytics beacon anywhere in it — searching the source for fetch, XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket or an HTTP library returns nothing.

The only traffic the app generates is the ad request made by Google's SDK, to Google's own ad servers, described in section 2.

The practical consequence is worth stating plainly, because it is the reason the app exists in this shape: put the phone in airplane mode and everything still works. Every unit, every regional কানি, every plot-area formula and the whole হিস্যা calculator are on the device. Standing in a field with no signal, the only thing you lose is the banner. That was the design goal, and it is why the calculation code has no network access to lose.

6. Libraries in the app

The app is built with React Native and bundles open-source libraries for navigation, animation, gesture handling, vector drawing, safe-area layout, local storage and clipboard access, along with the Bengali and Latin typefaces it renders text with. None of them contacts a server.

The one library that does use the network is Google Mobile Ads (react-native-google-mobile-ads, wrapping Google's play-services-ads). It is the subject of section 2 and is the only third party involved in this app at all.

There is no analytics SDK, attribution SDK, crash reporter, A/B testing framework, push-notification service or social-media SDK in this app.

Google's ads SDK ships alongside its own measurement components, which is why an unpacked build shows play-services-measurement classes. They are part of Google's ad-conversion measurement, not a separate analytics product the developer has added, configured or can read. No Firebase Analytics dependency is declared by this app, and no analytics data reaches the developer.

7. Clipboard

Tapping a result copies that number to your clipboard, so you can paste it into a message, a note or a calculator.

This only happens when you tap. The app never reads your clipboard — it only ever writes to it, and only the one number you tapped. Nothing is uploaded, and the developer never learns that you copied anything.

8. Android Auto Backup

Android's Auto Backup is switched off for this app (android:allowBackup="false"). Your region, saved units and preferences are therefore never copied to your Google account, never included in a phone-to-phone transfer, and exist only on the phone you set them on.

The trade-off is worth stating: a new phone starts fresh, and custom units you created have to be entered again. For a settings blob this small that is a fair price for the guarantee that no copy of it exists anywhere else.

9. Links that leave the app

The developer screen has two links to public profiles — LinkedIn and skrchowdhury.online. Tapping one asks Android to open that address in your browser.

The app hands the address to the operating system exactly as tapping a link in a message would. No identifier, no app data and nothing else about you is attached. From that point you are in your own browser, under the privacy policies of that site and that browser.

10. Retention and deletion

Everything the app saves is kept on your device for as long as you keep it, and nowhere else:

  • deleting a custom unit or resetting an adjusted value inside the app removes it immediately
  • Settings › Apps › জমি হিসাব › Storage › Clear data removes all of it at once
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of it

There is no deletion request to submit to the developer, because no copy exists off your device for anyone to delete. With Auto Backup disabled, there is not even a copy in your own Google account.

Data held by Google in connection with advertising is governed by Google's own retention and controls, not by the developer. Deleting your advertising ID, as described in section 2, is the control Android gives you over it.

11. Security

Your settings are protected by the Android application sandbox: the app's private storage is readable only by this app, and only on an unmodified device. There is no additional encryption layer, and none is claimed — on a rooted or compromised phone the sandbox guarantee is weaker, and that is true of every app.

The strongest protection here is structural rather than cryptographic. Your measurements are never written to storage and never transmitted, so there is no store to breach and no transfer to intercept. The ad request is made by Google's SDK over its own encrypted connection to Google.

12. Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR give you rights of access, correction, export and erasure over personal data a service holds about you. As far as the developer is concerned those rights are satisfied in the simplest possible way: no personal data is ever collected, so there is no profile to access, nothing to correct and nothing for anyone else to erase.

Where advertising is concerned, the counterpart controls are Android's and Google's, and they are real ones: reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, as described in section 2. Google's own account controls cover what it holds.

13. Verifying these claims

A privacy policy is a claim. These particular claims are testable, and you do not have to take the developer's word for any of them:

  • Airplane mode. Turn it on and use the app. Every unit conversion, plot-area calculation and হিস্যা split still works; only the banner is missing. Nothing you rely on was coming from a server.
  • Permissions. Open Settings › Apps › জমি হিসাব › Permissions. There is no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access to revoke, because none is requested. The Play listing shows the full declared list under About this app › App permissions › See more.
  • The build itself. With the Android SDK installed, aapt2 dump permissions app-release.apk prints the complete permission list of the shipped build — the eight entries in the table in section 4, plus the signature-level entry described under it, and nothing else.
  • The ad traffic. If you inspect the app's network traffic, every request goes to a Google ad host. There is no other destination, because there is no other server involved in this app.

14. Children

This app is not directed at children. Its Google Play target audience is set to 18 and over, because working out land shares and inheritance divisions is adult business, and the app is deliberately outside the Play Families programme.

The developer knowingly collects no data from anyone, children included. The app has no chat, no user-generated content and no in-app purchases. It does show advertising, which is the main reason the audience is set as it is.
