Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 August 2026

The short version: the app works entirely offline. It requests no Android permissions at all — not even internet access — so your grades cannot leave your device by any route the app controls. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What the app collects

Nothing is collected, because nothing is sent anywhere. The app has no server and no network permission. There is no login, no profile and no advertising.

What is stored on your device

So your work survives closing the app, the following is saved in the app's own private storage on your phone:

  • Courses, credits and grades you enter, and the results calculated from them
  • Saved calculation history
  • Grading scales, including any custom scale you create
  • Settings such as theme and language

This data belongs to you. It is readable only by this app, is never transmitted, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android Settings.

Android Auto Backup

The app allows Android's built-in Auto Backup. That is an operating-system feature, not part of this app: when it is enabled on your phone, Android may copy the app's saved data to your own Google Drive backup so it can be restored if you switch phones. The backup is held in your Google account under Google's terms, and neither the developer nor anyone else gains access to it.

To turn it off, use Android's own setting — typically Settings › Google › Backup, or the per-app backup control your device provides.

Files you export or share

The app can export your data to a file and can share a result as an image. Both happen only when you ask for them. When you do, you choose the destination through Android's own file picker or share sheet, and the file goes wherever you send it. What happens to it after that is governed by whichever app or service you chose, not by this policy. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

Reading files you pick

When restoring a backup, the app reads only the single file you select through the system document picker. It has no ability to browse your storage on its own.

Accuracy

Results depend on the grading scale selected and on the values you enter. Institutions differ in how they calculate CGPA and how they treat retakes and exclusions. Always confirm against your institution's official transcript or regulations before relying on a number for anything that matters.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.
