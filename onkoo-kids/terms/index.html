By installing or using Onkoo Kids you agree to these terms. The app is made for children, so these terms are addressed to the parent or guardian who installs it and lets a child use it; you accept them on the child's behalf. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

2. What the app is

Onkoo Kids is a free educational game: 40 maths games across 20 worlds, stickers, a garden and a parents' area with progress and certificates. It is designed to support early maths learning at home. It is not a replacement for a teacher, a school curriculum or a formal assessment, and the skill rings and certificates in the parents' area are encouragement, not grades.

Grown-ups can set a daily time limit and other settings in the parents' area. Deciding how much screen time suits a child is for the parent or guardian.

3. Tips

  • The whole game is free, with no ads and nothing to buy for children.
  • Grown-ups can leave an optional tip, in four fixed amounts, from the parents' area behind the parental gate. A tip is a voluntary contribution to the developer. It unlocks nothing and changes nothing in the game.
  • Tips are processed by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service. Prices are shown in your local currency before you confirm. Refunds are handled by Google Play according to its refund policy.
  • To prevent unwanted purchases, you can also require authentication for purchases in the Google Play app's settings.

4. Your data

Onkoo Kids collects no personal data. Progress, stickers, the garden, settings and any name typed on a certificate are kept only on the phone, and are erased by Reset progress, by clearing the app's data or by uninstalling the app. They are not backed up anywhere, so they cannot be restored once erased. The details are in the privacy policy.
