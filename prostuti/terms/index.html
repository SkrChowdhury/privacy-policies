2. Exam dates, syllabus and study guidance

The app's exam dates and paper-by-paper routines, the chapter lists for SSC, HSC, admission tests, the BCS preliminary and bank exams, and the marks distributions are compiled from the published notices and syllabuses of the education boards, NCTB, universities, the PSC and the recruiting banks. Dates are updated from a public sheet the developer maintains, and the app shows a countdown from them.

Boards and institutions change dates, units, venues and syllabuses, sometimes at short notice, and editions of textbooks differ. The app is therefore a study aid, not an official source:

  • always confirm exam dates, times, centres and the syllabus with your board's or institution's official notice and your admit card;
  • you can correct a date or edit the syllabus in the app to match your own notice or book, and you can report a mistake from the app;
  • the daily plan, the pace, the readiness figures and the revision schedule are suggestions worked out from what you mark. They do not guarantee any result.

6. Your data and Drive backup

Your exams, syllabus progress, routine, sessions, scores and notes are stored only on your phone (see the privacy policy). The developer cannot see or recover them. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes them.

With Pro you can back them up to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive and restore them on any phone. Restoring replaces the study data on the phone with the backup, after asking you. The backup is stored by Google in your account; keep that account secure, and note that the developer cannot restore a backup you have deleted.
