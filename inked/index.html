The short version: the developer collects nothing about you. There is no server behind Inked, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads. Every puzzle — including the daily print — is built into the app and checked on your phone.

Three things can leave your phone, and each is your choice. If you sign in to Google Play Games, your daily solve times and streaks are posted to its leaderboards. If you subscribe to Editions, Google Play handles the purchase. If you turn on cloud save, your progress is copied to your own Google Drive. None of it is ever sent to the developer.

1. What the developer collects

Nothing. There is no server behind Inked and no account of the developer's. The developer never receives:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail
  • your solves, times, mistakes, streaks or any other statistic
  • your location, at any precision
  • your device identifiers, Android ID or advertising ID
  • analytics, usage statistics, diagnostics or crash reports
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone

The puzzles, the daily print and every check of your work live inside the app on your phone. The optional Google services described in §3–§5 talk to Google, not to the developer.

2. What is stored on your device

So your progress survives closing the app, the following is saved in the app's private storage (key-value files under the app's private files/mmkv directory, which the Android sandbox makes unreadable to every other app):

What is savedDetail
ProgressFor each print you solve: your best time, mistakes, hints used, when you solved it and how often you replayed it. Per day: how many prints you solved and for how long. Your in-progress board, so you can continue where you left off
Daily printsWhich daily prints you have started, and which day's daily hint you have used
SettingsPaper, mistake mode, haptics, reduced motion, larger cells, left-handed controls, colourblind marks, the reminder and its time, and whether you have seen the introduction
Subscription statusA cached copy of whether Editions is active, so the app works offline. It is kept in its own file, never backed up and never exported, and is re-checked with Google Play
Play GamesLeaderboard scores waiting to be posted while offline, and whether you have been asked to sign in
Cloud save stateWhether cloud save is on, and when it last synced. Never your password

All of it is removed when you clear the app's data from Android Settings or uninstall the app.

3. Google Play Games leaderboards

Leaderboards are optional. Everyone in the world gets the same daily print, and if you sign in to Google Play Games, the app posts to its leaderboards:

  • your solve time for the day's print — your first attempt only
  • your total for the week (days solved and total time)
  • your streaks of consecutive daily solves

Play Games shows these next to your Play Games name and avatar, publicly, to other players on the same leaderboards. The app reads your Play Games name and the leaderboard rows to draw the Leaderboards screen. Nothing else is sent: never your progress, your other prints, your settings or your purchases.

The leaderboards are hosted by Google under Google's privacy policy. You can change your Play Games name, profile visibility or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or at play.google.com/store/account. If you never sign in, nothing is posted, and the game plays exactly the same.

4. Purchases

Inked is free. Editions is an optional subscription bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, your payment details and renewals; the developer never sees them. The app only asks Google Play whether a subscription is active, and acknowledges a new purchase as Google requires. You manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play.

5. Cloud save

Cloud save is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, your progress is copied to your own Google Drive, in the hidden folder only this app can see (Google's app data folder). The app asks for the single, non-sensitive drive.appdata permission, which gives no access to the rest of your Drive.

The file contains game progress only — solves, times, daily tallies, in-progress boards and settings — and never your subscription status, your email address or any identifier. To remove it, use Settings › Cloud save › Delete cloud save, or Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps. You can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. The daily reminder

The reminder is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, Android asks for permission to show notifications, and the app schedules one notification a day on the phone, at the time you choose. It names the day's print — for example its number and size — and is worked out on the phone. Nothing is fetched or sent to produce it.

It grants access to nothing. There is no storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone or advertising-ID permission.

8. Network access

Inked works fully offline. It goes online only for the Google services you use: Google Play (subscription checks), Google Play Games (when you are signed in) and Google Drive (when cloud save is on). There is no telemetry channel and no developer server to reach. If the network is unavailable, those features wait and the game is unaffected — leaderboard scores are queued and posted later.

9. Libraries in the app

Inked is built with React Native and bundles open-source libraries for drawing, animation, gestures, navigation, local storage, haptics, notifications and the time picker, plus Google's Play Billing and Play Games Services libraries. There is no advertising network, analytics SDK, attribution SDK, crash reporter or social-media SDK.

10. Android Auto Backup

When Android's own backup is enabled on your phone, it may copy Inked's progress and settings to your own Google account, so a new phone can restore them. Only the progress-and-settings file is included; the subscription status and cloud-save state are excluded by rule. The backup is held by Google under Google's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

11. Backup files and sharing prints

  • Export progress writes a readable .json file through Android's file picker, to wherever you choose. It never includes your subscription. Import reads only the one file you pick, and shows you what is in it before anything changes.
  • Share a print draws the image on your phone and hands it to Android's share sheet, saves it to Pictures/Inked, or copies the caption — only when you tap that button. Nothing is uploaded by the app.
  • The developer's LinkedIn and portfolio links open in your browser, only when you tap them.

12. Retention and deletion

  • Settings › Apps › Inked › Storage › Clear data, or uninstalling, removes everything on the phone
  • Cloud save data is deleted as described in §5
  • Leaderboard data is managed in Google Play Games (§3)

There is no deletion request to send the developer, because the developer holds no copy of your data.

13. Security

On the phone, your data is protected by the Android app sandbox. Everything that leaves the phone goes over HTTPS to Google's own services. The strongest protection is structural: there is no developer server holding your data, so there is nothing of the developer's to breach.

14. Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR give you rights of access, correction, export and erasure over personal data a service holds about you. The developer holds none. Your data on the phone is yours to export (§11) and erase (§12), and data held by Google services is managed through your Google account.

15. Children

Inked is a puzzle game rated for a general audience but not directed at children under 13. It shows no ads, has no chat and no user-generated content. The only public data — a Play Games name with leaderboard times — is shared only by players who choose to sign in to Play Games.

16. Changes to this policy

Any revision will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. If Inked ever began collecting something, this page would say so before the version that did it shipped.
