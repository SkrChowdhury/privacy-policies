2. What the app is

শব্দ খেলা is a free word game: a new Bangla word every day, six tries to find it, an archive of past puzzles and unlimited practice. Solved words come with a meaning and an example sentence to help you learn them.

Those meanings are written with care as a learning aid. They are short everyday glosses, not a dictionary of record, and may occasionally be incomplete or mistaken. If you find one that is wrong, please write to the address below. Do not rely on them for exams, translation or official use.
