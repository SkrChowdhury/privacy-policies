The short version: the game collects nothing about you. There is no server behind it, no account, no leaderboard, no analytics and no ads. Every puzzle is generated and checked on your phone.

Cloud save is the one exception, and it is off until you turn it on. With it off, the game never uses the network. With it on, your game progress — and nothing else — is copied to your own Google Drive so a new phone can restore it. The backup goes from your phone to your Drive; the developer has no copy of it and no way to read it. No name, no email address and no device identifier is ever put in it.

1. What the game collects

Nothing. Nothing is collected because there is nowhere for it to go: there is no server behind this game, no login of the developer's, no online leaderboard and no advertising.

Specifically, the game never collects, requests or receives:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail
  • your guesses, your statistics, your streak or your play times
  • your location, at any precision
  • your device identifiers, Android ID or advertising ID
  • analytics, usage statistics, diagnostics or crash reports
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone

The daily word, the word lists and the checking of every guess all live inside the app package on your phone. Nothing about the puzzle is fetched, and nothing about your play is reported.

Turning on cloud save does not change any of that. It copies your progress to your Google Drive — an account the developer has no access to. See §3.

2. What is stored on your device

So that your progress survives closing the game, the following is saved in the app's own private storage — a memory-mapped key-value store under the app's private files/mmkv directory, which the Android sandbox makes unreadable to every other app on the phone:

What is savedDetail
Puzzle progress The words you have guessed in each puzzle, kept per day and per difficulty so the daily, an archive puzzle and a practice game can all be in progress at once. Only the guessed words are stored — the coloured tile feedback is recalculated from them each time
Statistics Games played, games won, current streak, longest streak, guess distribution, and which day's puzzle you last completed
Collection and rewards The words you have collected with their meanings, your points balance and what you have spent it on, and the badges, titles and board skins you have earned
Settings Theme, language, difficulty, keyboard layout, high-contrast mode, reduce-motion preference, strict scoring, Bangla numerals, haptics, and whether you have seen the tutorial

This data belongs to you. It is readable only by this app, and it is removed when you reset your statistics in the game, clear the app's data from Android Settings, or uninstall the game. Uninstalling deletes all of it — unless you have turned cloud save on, in which case a copy also sits in your own Drive until you remove it there.

3. Cloud save

Cloud save is optional and off by default. You turn it on in Settings › ক্লাউডে সংরক্ষণ, and that screen tells you, before you do, that switching it on means the game is no longer fully offline.

Where it goes

To your own Google Drive, in two places:

  • a hidden folder that only this app can see, holding the document the game syncs from — Google calls this the app data folder
  • a file named shobdokhela-backup.json in your My Drive, which you can open, read, download, copy or delete like any other file of yours

The game asks Google for the narrowest access that allows this — two permissions, both classified by Google as non-sensitive:

Google Drive scopeWhat it allows
drive.appdata The hidden per-app folder, and nothing else. Only this app can see it
drive.file Files this app itself created — that is, the visible backup file

Neither grants any sight of the rest of your Drive. The game cannot read your documents, your photos or anything else stored there, and it never asks to.

Signing in shows the game your Google account's first name, so the settings row can tell you which account is linked. That stays on your device.

What is in the file

A single document containing game progress only: which puzzles you have solved and your guesses on them, your streak, statistics and guess distribution, your points balance and what you have spent it on, your collected words, badges, titles and board skins, and your settings.

What it does not contain: your name, your email address, any device or advertising identifier, your location, your contacts, or anything you have typed that is not a guess in this game.

The network is used only while backing up or restoring. Nothing is sent in the background, nothing is sent while you play, and none of it goes to advertising or analytics — there is neither in this game.

Turning it off, and deleting the backup

  • To unlink this phone, use Settings › অ্যাকাউন্ট সরিয়ে দিন. The game signs out and can reach nothing further; your backup stays in your Drive, which is the point of having one.
  • The visible copy: delete shobdokhela-backup.json from your My Drive.
  • The hidden copy: Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps › this app › Delete hidden app data.
  • To revoke the game's access to Drive entirely, remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Whatever sits in your Drive is held under Google's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it and receives no copy of it.

4. Permissions

The released build declares two real Android permissions:

PermissionWhy it is needed
android.permission.VIBRATE Haptic feedback on key presses and tile reveals. You can turn haptics off in Settings. It is an install-time permission that grants access to the vibration motor and to no information whatsoever.
android.permission.INTERNET Cloud save only — talking to Google Drive. Nothing opens a network connection until you turn cloud save on.

Neither grants access to personal information. INTERNET is an install-time permission and prompts nobody, which is exactly why this policy states plainly what it is for: the game is capable of reaching the network, and it does so only when you ask it to.

If you inspect the manifest of the released build you will also see com.skrchowdhury.shobdokhela.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. That entry is generated automatically by an Android system library. It is signature-level — only a build signed with this app's own key could ever hold it — it is scoped to this app alone, and it grants access to nothing. It exists so the game's internal message receivers are not exposed to other apps, which is a privacy protection rather than a privacy cost. It is listed here only so that the count matches what you actually see.

There is no storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone or notification permission.

5. Network access

With cloud save off — which is how the game ships — no network request is ever made. The word lists ship inside the app, the daily answer is derived on the device from the date, and guesses are validated against the bundled list. Put the phone in airplane mode and play: everything works, including tomorrow's puzzle.

With cloud save on, the game talks to Google Drive and nothing else, and only at the moments you would expect: when you sign in, when a backup is written, and when a restore is read. There is no background sync, no periodic upload and no telemetry channel. If the network is unavailable the backup simply fails and says so; play is unaffected.

6. Libraries in the game

The game is built with React Native and bundles open-source libraries for rendering, animation, gestures, navigation, local storage, haptics, translation, the system share sheet and the system file picker, along with a Bangla typeface. Cloud save adds Google's own sign-in library and ordinary HTTPS calls to the Google Drive API.

There is no advertising network, analytics SDK, attribution SDK, crash reporter, A/B testing framework, push-notification service or social-media SDK in this game. Apart from the Drive calls described in §3, no bundled library contacts a server at runtime.

7. Android Auto Backup

Separately from the game's own cloud save, the game allows Android's built-in Auto Backup. That is an operating-system feature rather than part of this app: when it is enabled on your phone, Android may copy the game's saved data to your own Google account, so your streak and statistics can be restored if you set up a new phone, or transferred directly during a phone-to-phone setup.

What is included is exactly the game's key-value store — statistics, settings, collection and in-progress puzzles. The word lists and caches are excluded, since they are already part of the app package and would only make the backup larger.

The backup is held in your Google account under Google's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it and receives no copy of it. To turn it off, use Android's own setting — typically Settings › Google › Backup, or the per-app backup control your device provides.

8. Backup files and sharing results

Two features can move data off your device by hand, and both require you to act:

  1. Export data. Writes your statistics, settings and puzzle progress to a .json file and hands it to Android's share sheet, so you can put it wherever you want — Drive, Files, a chat, an email to yourself.
  2. Share a result. Draws an image of your result grid and hands that image to the share sheet. The image shows the pattern of your guesses, not the answer.

In both cases you choose the destination, and the file goes only where you send it. What happens to it afterwards is governed by whichever app or service you chose, not by this policy. Nothing is uploaded automatically, and nothing is sent if you dismiss the share sheet.

The export is deliberately plain, readable JSON, with the format and export date at the top: it is your data, and a format you can open and inspect is a format you can trust. It is the same format cloud save writes, so a copy pulled off your Drive by hand goes straight back into the game.

9. Reading files you pick

When importing a backup, the game reads only the single file you select through the system document picker. It has no ability to browse your storage, list your files, or open anything you did not choose.

The file is validated before anything is changed: a file that is not a Shobdo Khela backup is rejected, and one written by a newer version of the game is refused outright rather than imported with unknown fields silently dropped.

10. Retention and deletion

Data is kept on your device for as long as you keep it, plus — if you turned cloud save on — in your own Drive until you remove it there. You are in full control of all of it:

  • Reset statistics in the game clears your stats, streak and guess distribution
  • Settings › Apps › শব্দ খেলা › Storage › Clear data removes everything on the phone at once
  • Uninstalling the game removes all of it from the phone
  • Your Drive copies are deleted by you, as described in §3

There is no deletion request to submit, because no copy exists anywhere the developer can reach. Every copy is either on your phone or in your own Google account.

11. Security

On the device, your data is protected by the Android application sandbox: the game's private storage is readable only by this app, and only on an unmodified device. There is no additional encryption layer, and none is claimed — on a rooted or compromised phone, the sandbox guarantee is weaker, and that is true of every app.

In transit, cloud save uses HTTPS to Google's own endpoints, and the sign-in token never leaves the device. At rest, the backup lives in your Google account under Google's security and your account's protections, including whatever two-factor authentication you have enabled there.

The strongest protection here remains structural rather than cryptographic: there is no server holding your data, so there is no breach of the developer's that could expose it.

12. Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR give you rights of access, correction, export and erasure over personal data a service holds about you. Those rights are fully satisfied here in the simplest possible way: no personal data is ever collected, so there is no profile to access, nothing to correct and nothing for anyone else to erase.

The equivalents live in the game itself and are always available to you — export gives you your data as a readable file, cloud save's own file in your Drive is yours to open and delete, and reset or uninstall erases what is on the phone.

13. Verifying these claims

  • Open Settings › Apps › শব্দ খেলা › Permissions. Vibration is the only entry that appears there; INTERNET is install-time and is not shown as a toggle.
  • Leave cloud save off and turn on airplane mode. Nothing stops working.
  • Turn cloud save on, back up, then look in your own Drive: the file is called shobdokhela-backup.json, and you can open it and read every field in it.
  • Check what the game is allowed to touch at myaccount.google.com/permissions — it will list the two Drive scopes in §3 and no others.
  • With the Android SDK installed, aapt2 dump permissions app-release.apk prints the build's complete permission list — VIBRATE and INTERNET plus the signature-level entry described above, and nothing else.

14. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included. It shows no ads, contains no in-app purchases, has no chat, no messaging, no online leaderboard and no user-generated content. The only ways it reaches outside itself are the share sheet, which you open yourself and which sends only the image you asked it to send, and cloud save, which is off unless someone turns it on and which writes only to that person's own Drive.
