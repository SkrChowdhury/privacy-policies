1. What the game collects

Nothing. Nothing is collected because nothing is sent anywhere. There is no server behind this game, no login, no online leaderboard and no advertising.

Specifically, the game never collects, requests or receives:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail
  • your guesses, your statistics, your streak or your play times
  • your location, at any precision
  • your device identifiers, Android ID or advertising ID
  • analytics, usage statistics, diagnostics or crash reports
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone

The daily word, the word lists and the checking of every guess all live inside the app package on your phone. Nothing about the puzzle is fetched, and nothing about your play is reported.

2. What is stored on your device

So that your progress survives closing the game, the following is saved in the app's own private storage — a memory-mapped key-value store under the app's private files/mmkv directory, which the Android sandbox makes unreadable to every other app on the phone:

What is savedDetail
Puzzle progress The words you have guessed in each puzzle, kept per day and per difficulty so the daily, an archive puzzle and a practice game can all be in progress at once. Only the guessed words are stored — the coloured tile feedback is recalculated from them each time
Statistics Games played, games won, current streak, longest streak, guess distribution, and which day's puzzle you last completed
Settings Theme, language, difficulty, keyboard layout, high-contrast mode, reduce-motion preference, strict scoring, Bangla numerals, haptics, and whether you have seen the tutorial

This data belongs to you. It is readable only by this app, is never transmitted, and is removed when you reset your statistics in the game, clear the app's data from Android Settings, or uninstall the game.

3. Permissions

The released build declares exactly one real Android permission:

PermissionWhy it is needed
android.permission.VIBRATE Haptic feedback on key presses and tile reveals. You can turn haptics off in Settings. It is an install-time permission that grants access to the vibration motor and to no information whatsoever.

If you inspect the manifest of the released build you will also see com.skrchowdhury.shobdokhela.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. That entry is generated automatically by an Android system library. It is signature-level — only a build signed with this app's own key could ever hold it — it is scoped to this app alone, and it grants access to nothing. It exists so the game's internal message receivers are not exposed to other apps, which is a privacy protection rather than a privacy cost. It is listed here only so that the count matches what you actually see.

There is no internet, storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone or notification permission.

4. Network access

The game makes no network requests, and the released build cannot make one: without INTERNET, the operating system itself refuses. The word lists ship inside the app, the daily answer is derived on the device from the date, and guesses are validated against the bundled list.

This is easy to confirm — put the phone in airplane mode and play. Everything works, including tomorrow's puzzle.

Internet access is declared only in the development build, so the bundler on a developer's own machine can serve code during development. It is not merged into the build published on Google Play.

5. Libraries in the game

The game is built with React Native and bundles open-source libraries for rendering, animation, gestures, navigation, local storage, haptics, translation, the system share sheet and the system file picker, along with a Bangla typeface.

There is no advertising network, analytics SDK, attribution SDK, crash reporter, A/B testing framework, push-notification service or social-media SDK in this game. No bundled library contacts a server at runtime; the released build has no permission that would let one.

6. Android Auto Backup

The game allows Android's built-in Auto Backup. That is an operating-system feature rather than part of this app: when it is enabled on your phone, Android may copy the game's saved data to your own Google account, so your streak and statistics can be restored if you set up a new phone, or transferred directly during a phone-to-phone setup.

What is included is exactly the game's key-value store — statistics, settings and in-progress puzzles. The word lists and caches are excluded, since they are already part of the app package and would only make the backup larger.

The backup is held in your Google account under Google's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it and receives no copy of it. To turn it off, use Android's own setting — typically Settings › Google › Backup, or the per-app backup control your device provides.

7. Backup files and sharing results

Two features can move data off your device, and both require you to act:

  1. Export data. Writes your statistics, settings and puzzle progress to a .json file and hands it to Android's share sheet, so you can put it wherever you want — Drive, Files, a chat, an email to yourself.
  2. Share a result. Draws an image of your result grid and hands that image to the share sheet. The image shows the pattern of your guesses, not the answer.

In both cases you choose the destination, and the file goes only where you send it. What happens to it afterwards is governed by whichever app or service you chose, not by this policy. Nothing is uploaded automatically, and nothing is sent if you dismiss the share sheet.

The export is deliberately plain, readable JSON, with the format and export date at the top: it is your data, and a format you can open and inspect is a format you can trust.

8. Reading files you pick

When importing a backup, the game reads only the single file you select through the system document picker. It has no ability to browse your storage, list your files, or open anything you did not choose.

The file is validated before anything is changed: a file that is not a Shobdo Khela backup is rejected, and one written by a newer version of the game is refused outright rather than imported with unknown fields silently dropped.

9. Retention and deletion

Data is kept on your device for as long as you keep it, and nowhere else. You are in full control of removing it:

  • Reset statistics in the game clears your stats, streak and guess distribution
  • Settings › Apps › শব্দ খেলা › Storage › Clear data removes everything at once
  • Uninstalling the game removes all of it

There is no deletion request to submit, because no copy exists off your device for anyone to delete. If you have Auto Backup enabled, remember that a copy may also exist in your own Google account until you remove it there.

10. Security

Your data is protected by the Android application sandbox: the game's private storage is readable only by this app, and only on an unmodified device. There is no additional encryption layer, and none is claimed — on a rooted or compromised phone, the sandbox guarantee is weaker, and that is true of every app.

The strongest protection here is structural rather than cryptographic: data that is never transmitted cannot be intercepted, and a build with no network permission cannot transmit it.

11. Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR give you rights of access, correction, export and erasure over personal data a service holds about you. Those rights are fully satisfied here in the simplest possible way: no personal data is ever collected, so there is no profile to access, nothing to correct and nothing for anyone else to erase.

The equivalents live in the game itself and are always available to you — export gives you your data as a readable file, and reset or uninstall erases it.

12. Verifying these claims

  • Open Settings › Apps › শব্দ খেলা › Permissions. Vibration is the only entry.
  • Turn on airplane mode and play. Nothing stops working.
  • With the Android SDK installed, aapt2 dump permissions app-release.apk prints the build's complete permission list — VIBRATE plus the signature-level entry described above, and nothing else.

13. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included. It shows no ads, contains no in-app purchases, has no chat, no messaging, no online leaderboard and no user-generated content. The only way it interacts with anything outside itself is the share sheet, which you open yourself and which sends only the image you asked it to send.
