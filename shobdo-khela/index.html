Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 August 2026

The short version: the game works entirely offline. It requests no Android permissions at all — not even internet access — so your play data cannot leave your device by any route the app controls. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What the app collects

Nothing is collected, because nothing is sent anywhere. The game has no server and no network permission. There is no login, no leaderboard account and no advertising. Puzzles are generated and checked on your device.

What is stored on your device

So your progress survives closing the game, the following is saved in the app's own private storage on your phone:

  • Your current puzzle and the guesses you have made in it
  • Statistics: games played, win rate, current and longest streak, guess distribution
  • Settings such as theme and language

This data belongs to you. It is readable only by this app, is never transmitted, and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data from Android Settings.

Android Auto Backup

The game allows Android's built-in Auto Backup. That is an operating-system feature, not part of this app: when it is enabled on your phone, Android may copy the game's saved data to your own Google Drive backup so your streak can be restored if you switch phones. The backup is held in your Google account under Google's terms, and neither the developer nor anyone else gains access to it.

To turn it off, use Android's own setting — typically Settings › Google › Backup, or the per-app backup control your device provides.

Backup files and sharing results

The game can export your statistics and history to a plain JSON file, and can share a result card as an image. Both happen only when you ask for them. You choose the destination through Android's own file picker or share sheet, and the file goes wherever you send it — what happens to it afterwards is governed by whichever app or service you chose, not by this policy. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

The export is deliberately plain, readable JSON: it is your data, and a format you can inspect is a format you can trust.

Reading files you pick

When importing a backup, the game reads only the single file you select through the system document picker. It has no ability to browse your storage on its own.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.
