Thakurghor terms

The short version: ঠাকুরঘর is a Bengali panjika and devotional companion. The panjika — tithi, nakshatra, festivals, fasts, paran and auspicious days — is calculated on your phone, and everything you keep in the app (reading progress, bookmarks and notes, japa counts, your sadhana log, family তিথি) stays on your phone. There is no account, no server and no analytics, and the developer receives nothing.

Two Google services are involved, and this page says exactly what each of them receives: Google AdMob, which shows the ads (and receives your device's advertising ID), and Google Play Billing, which handles membership and one-time purchases. If you choose to use your location, it is used once, on the phone, to pick the nearest city — the coordinates are never stored and never sent.

বাংলায় সংক্ষেপে: ঠাকুরঘরের পঞ্জিকা — তিথি, নক্ষত্র, উৎসব, উপবাস, পারণ ও শুভ দিন — আপনার ফোনেই গণনা হয়। আপনার পাঠ, বুকমার্ক ও নোট, জপের হিসাব, সাধনার তালিকা, পারিবারিক তিথি — সবকিছু ফোনেই থাকে। কোনো অ্যাকাউন্ট নেই, কোনো সার্ভার নেই; ডেভেলপার আপনার কোনো তথ্য পান না। বিজ্ঞাপন দেখাতে গিয়ে Google AdMob আপনার ডিভাইসের বিজ্ঞাপন আইডি পায়, আর কেনাকাটা হয় Google Play-এর মাধ্যমে — ডেভেলপার আপনার পেমেন্টের তথ্য দেখেন না। “আমার অবস্থান” চালু করলে আনুমানিক অবস্থান একবারই, ফোনের মধ্যেই, নিকটতম শহর বাছতে ব্যবহার হয় — অবস্থান কোথাও জমা বা পাঠানো হয় না। নিচের ইংরেজি অংশটিই পূর্ণ নীতি।

1. What the developer collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no server behind this app and no code in it that sends your data to the developer.

In particular, the developer never collects, requests or receives:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail
  • the names and dates you save in the family calendar (birthdays, শ্রাদ্ধ and anniversaries by তিথি)
  • what you read, bookmark, highlight or write notes on
  • your japa counts, sadhana log or puja checklists
  • your location, at any precision
  • analytics, usage statistics, diagnostics or crash reports
  • your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone

The panjika is not looked up from a server either: sunrise, sunset, tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, the festivals, the fasts and the auspicious days are all computed on the phone from astronomical formulas, for the city you pick. That is why the calendar works in airplane mode, and why using it reveals nothing about you to anyone.

3. Location

Location is optional and off by default. You can pick your city from a list instead, and the app works the same either way.

If you switch on আমার অবস্থান ব্যবহার করুন, Android asks for permission to use your approximate location. The app takes one coarse fix, finds the nearest city in its built-in list, and keeps only that city. The coordinates are discarded immediately: they are never saved, never sent anywhere, and never shared with the ads SDK or anyone else. The app does not declare or request precise location, and it does not track your location in the background.

4. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob in exactly four places: a banner on Home, and one ad card each in the festival, fasting and auspicious-day lists. Scripture, mantras, puja, japa, aarti and every other screen never show ads. You may also choose to watch a rewarded video ad, which turns ads off for a while. Membership removes ads altogether.

What Google receives

To select, serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, device information such as model, Android version and language, an approximate location derived from your IP address (not the location permission described in section 3), and standard ad events such as whether an ad was shown or tapped. This goes to Google, not to the developer, who sees only aggregate earnings in the AdMob dashboard. Google's description of how it uses data from apps that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What Google does not receive

The ad request carries no data from inside the app — not your city, your reading, your notes, your family days or your practice.

Turning ad personalisation off

In Settings › Google › All services › Ads, choose Delete advertising ID (on older versions, Opt out of Ads Personalisation). The app keeps working exactly as before.

5. Purchases

ঠাকুরঘর is free. Membership (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and one-time items — such as japa-mala styles, puja and audio packs, themes and the family calendar — are bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, your payment details and renewals; the developer never sees them. The app only asks Google Play which items your account owns, and acknowledges a new purchase as Google requires. You manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play (Settings › Payments & subscriptions).

6. Reminders and home-screen widgets

Reminders are optional. When you turn one on, Android asks for permission to show notifications, and the app schedules it on the phone: the evening before a festival or Ekadashi, the start of the paran window, a daily verse, weekly puja, your family days, and any শুভ দিন you rang the bell on. Their times are worked out on the phone from the same panjika. Nothing is fetched or sent to produce them. After a restart, the app re-schedules them, which is why it may run briefly when the phone starts.

Home-screen widgets are drawn on the phone from the same on-device data.

8. Network access

The panjika, the festivals, the Gita, the Chandi, the mantras and the kathas are all inside the app. Put the phone in airplane mode and every one of them still works.

The app's own code makes one kind of request, and only when you ask for it: downloading a text pack (the Veda and Upanishad translations) from the ডাউনলোড screen. The app then reads a small list of the available packs and the pack file itself from GitHub Pages (skrchowdhury.github.io), over HTTPS. The request carries nothing about you — no identifier, no account, no reading data; like any web server, GitHub sees the phone's IP address and the file asked for, under GitHub's privacy statement. By default packs download only on Wi-Fi.

The other traffic comes from Google's SDKs — the ads SDK and Google Play Billing.

9. Libraries in the app

The app is built with React Native and bundles open-source libraries for navigation, animation, storage, notifications, audio playback, home-screen widgets, sharing and on-device astronomy (Astronomy Engine). None of them contacts a server; the pack downloads in section 8 are the app's own code.

The two that use the network are Google Mobile Ads (section 4) and Google Play Billing (section 5). There is no analytics SDK, crash reporter, attribution SDK or social-media SDK in the app.

10. Sharing, copying and backup files

  • Sharing a verse or mantra as an image draws the card on the phone and hands it to Android's share sheet. Where it goes is your choice.
  • Copying a verse or the app link writes to your clipboard only when you tap; the app never reads your clipboard.
  • Backup (in আমার সংরক্ষণ) creates a file of your marks, notes, family days, sadhana, japa and katha progress and hands it to the share sheet, so you can save it to Google Drive or anywhere you like. The file is made only when you tap, and is never sent anywhere by the app.

11. Android Auto Backup

Android's Auto Backup is switched off (android:allowBackup="false"), so nothing from the app is copied to your Google account automatically. Use the Backup file in section 10 if you want a copy.

12. Links that leave the app

The app can open: its Google Play page, Google Play's subscription management, your email app (to write to the address below), and the developer's website and LinkedIn. Each is handed to Android exactly as tapping a link in a message would, with nothing about you attached.

13. Retention and deletion

Everything the app saves stays on your device until you remove it:

  • removing a bookmark, note or family day in the app deletes it at once
  • Settings › Apps › ঠাকুরঘর › Storage › Clear data removes all of it
  • uninstalling the app removes all of it

There is no deletion request to send the developer, because no copy exists anywhere else. Data held by Google for advertising or purchases is governed by Google's own controls.

14. Security

Your data is protected by the Android application sandbox. The strongest protection is structural: your notes, family days and practice are never transmitted, so there is no server to breach and no transfer to intercept. The app allows no unencrypted network traffic.

15. Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR give you rights of access, correction, export and erasure over personal data a service holds about you. The developer holds none. Your data on the phone is yours to export (section 10) and erase (section 13); data held by Google is managed through your Google account and the ad controls in section 4.

16. Children

ঠাকুরঘর is a devotional app for a general audience, but it is not directed at children under 13: it shows ads and offers purchases. It has no chat and no user-generated content shared with others, and the developer knowingly collects no data from anyone, children included.
