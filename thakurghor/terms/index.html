2. The panjika and religious guidance

The app calculates tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise and sunset, festivals, fasts, paran windows, muhurtas and auspicious days on your phone, by two reckonings: গুপ্তপ্রেস (Surya Siddhanta) and বিশুদ্ধ সিদ্ধান্ত (modern drik calculation). The results are checked against published almanacs, and almanacs themselves differ between systems, regions and editions.

The app is therefore a guide, not religious authority. Festival dates, Ekadashi and paran times, and above all the dates and lagnas for marriage, griha-pravesh, annaprashan, upanayana and other sacraments should be confirmed with your family purohit or the panjika your family follows. The developer is not responsible for decisions made on the app's calculations alone.
